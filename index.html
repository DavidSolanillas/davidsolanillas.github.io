Contact models and robot learning

I benchmarked our LCP-based bristle-friction models against MuJoCo on grasping and non-prehensile manipulation. Relaxed complementarity in convex models doesn’t capture stick-slip transitions or true sticking. A published sim-to-real benchmark had to add a virtual wall to make one task trainable; our model reproduces it with friction alone.

Rigid bodies as four point masses

Each link is modelled by four point masses. The mass matrix becomes constant, the nonlinear inertial terms disappear, and constraint Jacobians are linear in the generalized coordinates. For differentiable physics, the expensive terms vanish or can be precomputed: ∂M/∂q = 0, ∂c/∂q = 0, and constant Hessians for the constraint Jacobians.
